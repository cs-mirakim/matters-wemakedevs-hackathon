How Matters Guarantees Zero Amnesia & Record Integrity:

In traditional relational databases, UPDATE or DELETE operations erase past agreement history. In Matters, FalkorDB's bi-temporal graph engine never mutates historical states. Every commitment update is marked with valid_to when a superseding decision is made. This guarantees transparent auditability (who, when, and why scope changed).
